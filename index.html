Our Center Services

  • ✅ Five Daily Prayers: Always Open. Iqamah intervals: Fajr 20 Min; Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib & Isha 15 Min (30 Min for Isha during Ramadan).
  • ✅ Weekend Classes: Saturday & Sunday for Arabic, Islamic Studies & Quran.
  • ✅ Recreation Center: Sports activities and community engagement.
  • ✅ Religious Counseling: Spiritual guidance and counseling.
  • ✅ Islamic Weddings & Functions: Facilities and coordination.
  • ✅ Dawah: Outreach and support for non-Muslims.
  • ✅ Funeral Services: Funeral room at the Islamic Center of Lumberton in correlation with Muhajreen Cemetery.
